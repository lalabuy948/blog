About

Written by DP 1 ☕️

Founder & CEO of 1703 Group · Elixir engineer

I run 1703 Group, an Elixir/BEAM engineering boutique in Luxembourg. We build fault-tolerant, real-time, scalable systems, do zero-downtime legacy rewrites, and place Elixir talent. I still write the code myself.

Recent work is in broadcast. I worked on CyanCamera, an iPhone broadcast camera app used in productions for a big tech company, and on CyanView Studio (LiveLab), a desktop color studio that ran on a major artist’s tour and a big fashion show in 2026.

Over 10 years leading critical projects: zero-downtime ERP migrations, live system integrations serving millions of users, cross-department transformations. Delivered 2x engineering efficiency in 6 months and 45% tech debt reduction in 1.5 years.

Keynote at Code BEAM Europe 2025: CyanView & Elixir in Broadcasts, with David Bourgeois. Speaker at RTCON and Goatmire (2026). Author of PhoenixAnalytics.

Based in Luxembourg · Operate Remote · Open to on-site visits
